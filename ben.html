Got Questions?

Everything You Need to Know Before We Talk

We believe in complete transparency and open communication. Here are the answers to the questions we hear most often.

How much do your services cost?

+
Every manuscript is as unique as the author who wrote it — and so is every solution we create. We do not believe in one-size-fits-all pricing because we do not believe in one-size-fits-all service. After a brief, free consultation where we learn about your manuscript, your goals, and your specific needs, we will craft a fully tailored proposal designed specifically for your book. Reach out to start that conversation — it costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

How long will the process take?

+
Timeline varies depending on the length of your manuscript, the depth of service selected, and the current scheduling of our team. We always prioritize quality above speed — because rushing excellence is a contradiction in terms. During our initial consultation, we will discuss your publishing timeline and work with you to create a schedule that delivers the highest quality within your timeframe.

Will you change my writing voice?

+
Never. Your writing voice is the soul of your book — it is the unique fingerprint that makes your work distinctly yours, and it is something we hold sacred. Our entire approach is built around amplifying your voice, not replacing it. Every edit, every refinement, every suggestion we make is filtered through one guiding principle: does this make the author's voice stronger and clearer?

What genres do you work with?

+
We work with every genre across both fiction and non-fiction. Fiction romance, fantasy, thriller, mystery, science fiction, historical fiction, literary fiction, horror, young adult, middle grade, and children's books. Non-fiction self-help, business, memoir, biography, health and wellness, personal development, spirituality, and more. If you wrote it — regardless of genre, length, or subject matter — we have the expertise to elevate it.

Do I need all of your services or can I choose individual ones?

+
You can absolutely choose individual services based on your specific needs. Some authors come to us needing only formatting. Others need a deep editing overhaul. Others want the complete Full Transformation Package. During our consultation, we will honestly assess your manuscript and recommend only what will genuinely benefit your book — nothing more, nothing less.

How many revisions do I get?

+
We work with you until you are completely, unreservedly satisfied with the result. We do not put an arbitrary cap on the collaborative process because we believe the goal — a manuscript you are bursting with pride to share with the world — is more important than any round limit.

How do you handle confidentiality?

+
Your manuscript is your intellectual property and we treat it with the utmost confidentiality and respect. All manuscripts submitted to our platform are protected under strict non-disclosure agreements. We will never share, reference, publish, or discuss your work with anyone outside of the team members directly involved in your project.

Do you work with first-time authors?

+
Absolutely — and we love working with first-time authors. There is something incredibly exciting about helping a debut author bring their very first book into the world at the highest possible level. We provide extra guidance, patience, and support for authors navigating the publishing process for the first time.

What formats will I receive my completed manuscript in?

+
You will receive your completed manuscript in whatever formats best suit your publishing plans. This typically includes a tracked-changes document, a clean final document, a print-ready PDF, and eBook files (ePub and Mobi) if formatting is included. We ensure compatibility with Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Draft2Digital, and more.

How do I get started?

+
Simply click any "Start Your Transformation" button on this page, fill in our short contact form, and tell us about your book. We respond to every inquiry within 24 hours — not with an automated message, but with a genuine, personalized response from a real member of our team. The first conversation is always free.
